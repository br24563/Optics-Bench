How the physics works

Each lens uses the classic thin-lens graphical construction: a ray through the optical center is never bent, so every ray parallel to it must refract to cross the same point on the focal plane. That's the only rule driving every bend you see — no lookup tables, no fake bending.

Mirrors use plain specular reflection (angle of incidence = angle of reflection). Positive focal length draws a convex (converging) lens; negative draws a concave (diverging) one.
